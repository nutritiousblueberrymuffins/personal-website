About me

A CPA who also ships code.

I work in accounting, where I care about systems that balance, records that can be audited, and work that someone else can pick up later. I bring the same habits to the software I write.

Alongside my accounting work, I build careful apps in JavaScript and TypeScript, and I keep track of both on this site, one entry on the thread at a time.
