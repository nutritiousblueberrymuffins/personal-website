About me

A CPA who learned to ship code.

I spent the first years of my career in accounting, where I learned to care about systems that balance, records that can be audited, and work that someone else can pick up later. Those habits came with me when I started writing software.

Today I build small, careful web apps in JavaScript and TypeScript, and I document the journey on this site, one entry on the thread at a time.
